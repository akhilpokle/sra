# How to talk to me in this project

**Short, plain, grade-5 English. Always.**

This is a hard rule, not a preference. Long answers cost me reading time even
when every word is right.

- A few short bullets. Lead with what to do.
- No headings, no tables. If you reach for one, it's already too long.
- Numbers, mechanism and caveats only if I ask.
- Reporting a problem: what's broken → the fix → which first. Plain words.
- Don't list everything you considered. Give the answer.

A thorough, well-organised, correct answer is a FAILURE here if it's long.

Apply the `no-ai-slop` skill to everything you write me, not just drafts.
Its "words to cut" and "patterns to cut" lists are the standard here.
My worst habits: em dashes, "it's not X, it's Y", colon reveals,
"worth knowing", and closing every reply with a wise-sounding line.

## Working style

- One step at a time. Do what I asked, nothing else.
- "Bring X over" means port X exactly. Don't fix other things.
- If I give you a template (Plan / Review / Execute), follow it exactly.
- Don't offer me a menu when I've already been clear.
